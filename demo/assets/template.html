01 How it works

⟳

Review as an MDP

The paper is built as an environment; the review log is the state the agent carries between steps. A review is a trajectory through it, not one pass of generation.

⇄

Two kinds of action

read, lookup and finish act on the paper. log, update and outline act on the log. Every step is one or the other — which is what the recorded run replays.

◎

The log drives the reading

What is unsettled decides where it looks next, so it does not read front to back. Every claim must reach a verdict, every question an answer — or a confirmed absence, itself a finding.

02 Why it is different

Passive generation

Read the paper front to back, then write down your impressions. Nothing anchors a criticism to anything; the reader has to take it on trust.

Investigation

Extract what the authors claim, write down what you doubt, then go looking. The open questions decide where you read next, and the review is assembled from what you settled.

Every point in the final review is assembled from entries the agent settled — and every one of them links back to the evidence that settled it.

Nothing in the review is asserted without a record of where it came from. The recorded run below lets you click any point and follow it back.

03 The review log

The agent's working memory is three kinds of entry. It is the whole mechanism — everything else follows from keeping it honest.

C

claims

Assertions the authors made. Each must reach a verdict: supported, weak, or invalid.

Q

questions

The agent's own uncertainties. Each must be answered — or confirmed genuinely unanswerable, which is itself a finding. An absence is questioned, never asserted.

N

notes

Its own observations: patterns across tables, things worth tracking as it reads.
