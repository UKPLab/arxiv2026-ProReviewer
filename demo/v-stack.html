The idea

Passive generation

Read the paper front to back, then write down your impressions. Nothing anchors a criticism to anything; the reader has to take it on trust.

Investigation

Extract what the authors claim, write down what you doubt, then go looking. The open questions decide where you read next, and the review is assembled from what you settled.

The review log

The agent's working memory is three kinds of entry. It is the whole mechanism — everything else follows from keeping it honest.

C claims
Assertions the authors made. Each must reach a verdict: supported, weak, or invalid.
Q questions
The agent's own uncertainties. Each must be answered — or confirmed genuinely unanswerable, which is itself a finding. An absence is questioned, never asserted.
N notes
Its own observations: patterns across tables, things to track.

A recorded run

The replay is derived from the archived transcript, not re-enacted. The build refuses to publish a run that does not reproduce the session's own review_log.json.
